Causal reading · source attribution preserved

The same knob pulls two latency metrics apart

configurationSmaller token budget

A long prefill is split into more, shorter scheduler steps.

→
scheduler effectDecode gets shorter interruptions

Current vLLM scheduling prioritizes pending decode work before filling the remaining budget with prefill.

→
observed directionTail ITL falls

In the issue, P99 ITL drops from 400.31 ms with no chunking to 227 ms at 3K.

Why streaming becomes smoother

Shorter prefill chunks limit how long decode work waits behind a prefill step. vLLM documents smaller budgets as the direction for better ITL.

Why first token becomes slower

More chunks add scheduling and KV-history access work. The issue author gives this mechanism; current vLLM guidance independently says larger budgets favor TTFT.

The benchmark supports the latency directions. It does not isolate how much of the TTFT change comes from scheduling, KV access, kernels, or another correlated factor.

Replacement · controlled English

A useful answer says what the table cannot decide

Corrected operator summary

Do not select 3K from this table alone. The 3K setting has the lowest reported P99 ITL, but its average TTFT is 1,445 ms. Thus, it misses the stated 1,300 ms TTFT SLO.

The 5K setting is the only chunked setting below that TTFT limit, at 1,299 ms. It also has a lower reported P99 ITL than no-chunk, 297 ms versus 400.31 ms. However, the issue does not give a tail-ITL SLO, throughput, request count, or uncertainty. Therefore, the data does not prove that 5K is the production setting.

Use a controlled load test to compare 5K and nearby budgets at the target arrival rate. Define each percentile population before the test. Evaluate disaggregated prefill if tail ITL needs independent control, but do not claim a throughput gain: current vLLM documentation explicitly excludes that benefit.

Metric definitionsPer-token gap, per-request maximum, or another population?
Load conditionsArrival rate, concurrency, duration, warm-up, and request count.
Decision limitsA tail-ITL SLO and an allowed violation rate, not TTFT alone.
Capacity resultThroughput at the SLO, plus confidence intervals or repeated runs.
